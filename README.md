# Tech Support Ticket Template

This repository contains adaptable templates for submitting common IT support tickets and structured service requests. The templates help users provide the information support teams need for consistent triage, approval, fulfilment and resolution.

## Purpose

These templates provide structured formats for staff to report technical issues or request IT services. They are intended to:

- Improve the efficiency and effectiveness of IT support teams.
- Ensure that issues and requests are documented consistently.
- Provide clear communication between requesters and support teams.
- Collect the information needed for troubleshooting, approval or fulfilment.
- Support tracking through the organisation's chosen ticketing system.

## Choosing a Template

Use one of the general support tiers for faults, unexpected behaviour and ordinary technical assistance. Use a specialist template when the request requires a different approval, security or fulfilment workflow.

### General Support Tickets

The general support templates provide progressively more detailed levels of ticket logging. They intentionally share core fields so that essential information is collected consistently at every tier.

| Template | Intended Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Super Simple](Templates/1.%20General%20Support%20Tickets/1.%20Super%20Simple%20Template/Super-Simple-Template.txt) | Small teams or environments needing a quick, consistent support request. |
| [Expanded Simple](Templates/1.%20General%20Support%20Tickets/2.%20Expanded%20Simple%20Template/Expanded-Simple-Template.txt) | General service desks needing requester details and additional device information. |
| [Detailed](Templates/1.%20General%20Support%20Tickets/3.%20Detailed%20Template/Detailed-Template.txt) | Larger, multi-site or structured support environments requiring categorisation, user impact and escalation information. |

The requester records the urgency and impact of the issue. The support team should assign the final priority according to the organisation's triage process.

### Specialist Tickets

| Template | Intended Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Application, Website and AI Service Request](Templates/2.%20Application%20Website%20and%20AI%20Service%20Request/Application-Website-and-AI-Service-Request.txt) | Assessment and approval of a new application, website, online service or AI capability. |
| [Access and Permissions Request](Templates/3.%20Access%20and%20Permissions%20Request/Access-and-Permissions-Request.txt) | Adding, changing, removing or temporarily granting access to an existing resource. |
| [Cybersecurity and Privacy Incident Report](Templates/4.%20Cybersecurity%20and%20Privacy%20Incident%20Report/Cybersecurity-and-Privacy-Incident-Report.txt) | Reporting suspected compromise, phishing, malware, data exposure or another security event. |
| [Joiner, Mover and Leaver Request](Templates/5.%20Joiner%20Mover%20and%20Leaver%20Request/Joiner-Mover-and-Leaver-Request.txt) | Coordinating account, access, equipment and ownership changes caused by an employment lifecycle event. |

Use the Joiner, Mover and Leaver template instead of a standalone Access and Permissions request when the access change is part of an employment change.

## How to Use

1. Select the template that matches the required workflow.
2. Copy and customise the template for your organisation.
3. Replace all bracketed placeholders, categories, channels, timeframes and approval roles.
4. Publish it through your chosen ticketing system, form, email process or service portal.
5. Review the deployed template periodically as services and support processes change.

## Customisation Notes

- Align urgency, escalation and approval fields with your organisation's actual processes.
- Remove fields that your ticketing platform fills automatically.
- Use conditional sections where your platform supports them.
- Do not ask users to submit passwords, MFA codes or recovery codes.
- Direct sensitive information and evidence through an approved secure channel.
